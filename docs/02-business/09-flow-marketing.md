# Flow Marketing

## Strategi Marketing Digital

### SEO
- Optimasi konten blog dengan keyword umroh
- Meta tags & structured data
- Sitemap otomatis
- Breadcrumb navigation
- Image optimization

### Konten Marketing
- Artikel blog informatif (panduan, tips, berita)
- Testimoni jamaah
- Galeri foto & video
- Promo & diskon

### Social Media Integration
- WhatsApp share button
- WhatsApp registration flow
- Social media links

### Lead Management
- Registrasi WhatsApp terintegrasi
- Tracking sumber lead
- Follow up otomatis (future)
- CRM integration (future)

## Metrik Marketing

| Metrik | Deskripsi |
|--------|-----------|
| Website Visitors | Jumlah pengunjung |
| Lead Generated | Jumlah registrasi |
| Conversion Rate | Lead ke booking |
| Bounce Rate | Persentase pentalan |
| Avg Session Duration | Durasi kunjungan |
